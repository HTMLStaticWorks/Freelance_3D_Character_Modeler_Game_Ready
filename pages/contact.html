Quick Answers

What files should I send?
Concept art (PNG/PSD/PDF), reference images, any technical brief (poly budget, engine, LOD requirements). The more detail, the better the quote.
How long does a character take?
A typical Professional package takes 3–4 weeks. Rush delivery is available for +40%. I'll give you an exact timeline in the quote.
Do you work with NDA agreements?
Yes, I sign NDAs before starting any project. All work is confidential until you choose to publish it.
What payment methods do you accept?
50% deposit upfront, 50% on delivery. I accept bank transfer, PayPal and Stripe. Milestone payments available for Studio packages.
