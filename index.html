How It Works

The Modeling Pipeline

A transparent, stage-gated workflow with dashboard checkpoints at every milestone.

Step 01

Concept Art Upload

Studio uploads reference sheets, concept art and technical brief via the dashboard. I review and confirm scope, poly budget and timeline.

Day 1–2
Step 02

High-Poly Sculpt Review

High-resolution ZBrush sculpt uploaded to dashboard as turntable render and wireframe. Studio approves silhouette and detail level before retopo begins.

Day 3–7
Step 03

Retopology & Wireframe Check

Game-ready low-poly mesh built with clean edge loops. Wireframe uploaded for poly-count verification and topology approval.

Day 8–12
Step 04

UV Unwrap & Baking

Efficient UV layout, normal map baking from high to low. Texel density report included for quality assurance.

Day 13–16
Step 05

PBR Texture Showcase

Full texture set rendered in Marmoset Toolbag and uploaded. Studio reviews material quality and requests adjustments via revision rounds.

Day 17–21
Step 06

Rigging, LODs & Final Delivery

Full rig, weight painting, LOD chain generated. Final asset package available to download from dashboard with all formats and maps.

Day 22–28
